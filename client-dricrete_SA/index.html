The Dricrete way

Hydrophilic technology.

Dricrete is a time-proven waterproofing solution that amplifies your protection over time. The autogenous crack-sealing technology happens by the absorption and use of water to build a protective crystalline structure — healing up any cracks or pores in your structure.

The result? Significant Value Engineering dollars — and weeks of time saved.

Why does Dricrete waterproof better than competitors?

At the core of Dricrete's success is our proprietary crystalline waterproofing technology. While most other waterproofing methods are hydrophobic — attempting to repel water right at the surface — our hydrophilic technology actually harnesses water as a catalyst.

Most methods — hydrophobic

Fights water at the surface

A coating or membrane tries to repel water where it lands. Every seam, lap and puncture is a place for that fight to be lost.

Dricrete — hydrophilic

Uses water as the catalyst

Water is what triggers the reaction. The more it finds a way in, the more crystal grows to close the path behind it.

How crystalline waterproofing works

When a cement particle hydrates, it becomes a hard, solid mass. Crystalline waterproofing adds a second set of chemicals to the mixture that continue to react.

Step 01

The cement hydrates

Cement particles hydrate and lock together into a hard, solid mass — ordinary concrete, with ordinary porosity.

Step 02

A second set of chemicals reacts

Dricrete's chemistry keeps reacting after the concrete has set, seeking out moisture wherever it sits in the matrix.

Step 03

A needle-like structure forms

The end product is a non-soluble, needle-like structure — essentially, waterproof armor grown inside your concrete.
