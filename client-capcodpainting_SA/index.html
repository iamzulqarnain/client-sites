Our process

No mystery. No surprise invoice.

Pick a service to see exactly how the job runs, start to finish.

Walkthrough & written quote

We walk the whole exterior with you, flag rot and problem areas, and put a fixed price in writing.

Wash, scrape & repair

Pressure or soft wash, scrape failing paint, replace rotted boards, sand and caulk every seam.

Prime & two coats

Spot or full prime, then two coats of salt-air-rated coating in your chosen color.

Punch list & sign-off

We walk it again together. Nothing is final until you've pointed at every detail you want fixed.

Color & sheen consult

Sample boards in your own light before you commit to a gallon.

Protect the house

Furniture moved and covered, floors papered, doorways sealed, dust contained.

Patch, sand, cut & roll

Holes filled, surfaces sanded smooth, crisp lines cut by hand — two coats standard.

Clean, replace, review

Everything back where it was, floors vacuumed, and a room-by-room walkthrough.

Surface assessment

Shingle, roof, vinyl, brick and stone each get a different pressure and solution.

Plants & fixtures protected

Beds pre-wet and covered, outlets and lights sealed, windows checked.

Wash & dwell

Soft wash where pressure would damage, controlled pressure where it won't.

Full rinse down

House, walks and plantings rinsed clean. No chalky residue left behind.

FAQs

The questions everyone asks

How fast can I get an estimate?
Usually within 24 hours of your call or form. Most estimates take 30–45 minutes on site, and you get a written fixed price — not a range.
How long does an exterior repaint take?
A typical Cape-style home runs 5–9 working days depending on prep, rot repair, and weather. We give you a day-by-day plan before we start and tell you immediately if rain moves it.
When is the best time of year to paint on the Cape?
Exteriors: late April through October, when temps and humidity cooperate. Interiors: any month — winter is actually our best availability and pricing.
Do you do the prep work, or just paint?
Prep is the job. Washing, scraping, sanding, caulking, priming, and rot repair are all included and itemized in your quote. A paint job without prep fails in two to three years here.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — fully licensed and insured, with certificates available on request before work begins.
Can you help pick colors?
Yes. We bring samples, paint test boards, and look at them in your actual light at different times of day. Cape light is its own thing.
